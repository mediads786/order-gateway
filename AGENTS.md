# Evidence and publication rules

Read `docs/verification-summary.md` before evaluating portfolio claims or proposing another verification run. It records completed tests, dates, provenance and limits. Consult `docs/publication-review-2026-10-17.md` for the repository disclosure review and `SECURITY.md` before publishing.

The owner's real Shopify development-store order reached confirmed Odoo on 15 October 2026. Preserve that evidence. Do not describe the earlier synthetic test as the only test, or erase a historical success because a service is stopped today. Re-test only when a change, failure or unresolved question warrants it; explain the specific gap first. Do not invent missing check counts or broaden one successful fixture into a universal guarantee.

Gmail and WhatsApp belong to the separate n8n implementation. Read that repository's `docs/NOTIFICATION-VERIFICATION.md`: the owner reports activation and testing after Meta template approval. A later inactive workflow snapshot does not invalidate that historical test.

Before each commit or upload, enumerate the exact proposed files, inspect their complete content and staged diff, scan for secrets and private endpoints with redacted output, and inspect every new image/recording plus metadata. Never publish raw credentials, environment files, database exports, execution payloads, private QA originals, live deployment IPs/hostnames, customer contacts or account identifiers. Do not force-add ignored evidence. Preserve originals privately and publish a separate sanitized summary. Ignore rules and scanner passes are safeguards, not proof of zero security risk.

Do not send or retry orders, activate workflows, send notifications, rotate credentials, or change a deployment merely to reconcile existing documentation.
